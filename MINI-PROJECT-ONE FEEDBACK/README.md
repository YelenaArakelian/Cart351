# CART 351 – Mini Project One Feedback

## Project 1: [Face de book]

[Kerven]

### Description

It is an interactive application showing its users how easily information typed inside a browser can quickly be used against you.

### What Worked Well

I liked how quickly well it worked, the website gathered the data effectively well, and peformed as how the surface intended to. I liked also how it raises awarness to how you should be careful of where you put your infornation on the web. As a victim of scams, I found this expermient crucial.

### What Could Be Improved

I understand that this type of experience happens quickly, but I wish there was a bit more interaction involved. For example, the user could be given different choices or warning signs to look out for before the scam is revealed. I think this would make the experience more engaging while also helping users better understand how easily they can fall for online scams. But overall, I still find it good.

### Suggestions

One suggestion would be to make the scam reveal more interactive. For example, when the website tells users they have been hacked, it could show different scenarios of what could happen to their stolen information, such as someone accessing their accounts or using their personal details. I think this would make the experience more impactful and help users understand the consequences of online scams.
